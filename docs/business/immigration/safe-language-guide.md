# Safe Language Guide For Business Visitor Records

Private drafting guide. Not legal or immigration advice.

## Use This Framing

- Temporary business consultation.
- Strategic planning.
- Coordination meetings.
- Review of Dominican Republic-based operations.
- Meetings with business associates.
- Internal planning sessions.
- Systems review and training discussion.
- Return to the Dominican Republic after the temporary visit.
- No activity requiring a different legal status or authorization.

## Avoid This Framing

Avoid language that suggests:

- An open-ended stay.
- Relocation to the United States.
- Employment or labor physically performed in the United States.
- Daily operations being run from the United States.
- Customer-support shifts from the United States.
- Cleaning, maintenance, guest services, or property labor in the United States.
- Payment of wages for activities requiring a different legal status or authorization.
- Any wording that sounds like yearly admission or a specific period of admission is assured.

## Preferred Diana Description

Diana Sori Garcia Bautista is a Dominican Republic-based Founding Operations Pillar and Dominican Republic Operations Lead of MLADIS Bookings. She supports Dominican Republic-based vacation-rental operations and may be invited to the United States temporarily for consultation, strategy, planning, systems review, and meetings with business associates or professional advisors.

## Annual Visit Language

Safe:

> MLADIS LLC may invite Diana Sori Garcia Bautista from time to time for temporary business consultation and planning.

Avoid:

> MLADIS requires Diana to be physically present in the United States every year.

The first sentence describes a possible temporary business purpose. The second sounds too fixed, operationally necessary, and broad.
