# MLADIS Business Visit Policy

Private internal policy. Not legal or immigration advice.

## Policy

MLADIS LLC may invite non-U.S. business collaborators for temporary business visits only when the purpose is consultation, strategy, planning, contract negotiation, training discussion, coordination, or meetings with business associates or professional advisors.

Business visitors may not perform employment, hands-on operations, customer-service shifts, cleaning, maintenance, paid labor, or productive work physically in the United States unless they have the correct legal status or authorization.

## Required Record For Each Visit

Every business visit should have:

1. A written agenda.
2. Expected arrival and departure dates.
3. A clear business purpose.
4. Confirmation that ordinary operational activities remain based outside the United States.
5. Confirmation that the visitor will comply with visa and I-94/admission limits.
6. Proof that the visitor is expected to depart after the temporary visit.

## Escalate To Counsel

Pause and seek qualified immigration counsel before using this policy if there is any prior overstay, visa refusal, removal issue, unauthorized labor concern, criminal issue, planned long visit, planned compensation issue, or activity that does not clearly fit temporary business consultation.
