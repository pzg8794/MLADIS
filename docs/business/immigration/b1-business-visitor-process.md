# B-1 Business Visitor Process For MLADIS Bookings

Private process guide. Not legal or immigration advice. Use qualified immigration counsel if there is any prior overstay, visa refusal, removal issue, unauthorized labor, criminal issue, or other admissibility concern.

## Purpose

MLADIS LLC may invite Diana Sori Garcia Bautista from time to time for temporary business visitor purposes connected to MLADIS Bookings. The business purpose should be consultation, planning, strategy, systems review, business-development discussion, and meetings with business associates or professional advisors.

The annual planning goal is an internal business cadence only. It is not a guarantee that a visa will be issued, that admission will be granted, or that a particular length of stay will be allowed.

## Safe Activity Lane

Use this process only when the planned activities fit a temporary business-visitor lane:

- Consult with Piter Zacari Garcia Bautista and MLADIS business associates.
- Review MLADIS Bookings workflows and operating standards.
- Plan Dominican Republic-based property operations.
- Discuss guest-experience procedures and customer communication standards.
- Review reporting, booking, invoice, deposit, and admin workflows.
- Meet with accountants, lawyers, platform vendors, lenders, or other business advisors if needed.
- Return to the Dominican Republic after the temporary visit.

Do not use this process for hands-on guest services, cleaning, maintenance, United States customer-support shifts, or any activity that should be handled under a different legal status or authorization.

## Step 1 - Pre-Screen Visa History

Before preparing a specific invitation letter, complete the private visa-history checklist outside Git:

- Prior visa type and expiration date.
- Last United States entry date.
- Last I-94 admitted-until date.
- Actual departure date.
- Prior visa refusal, overstay, removal/deportation issue, or unauthorized labor concern.

If any answer suggests a prior overstay or serious immigration issue, pause and ask an immigration attorney before filing or traveling.

## Step 2 - Define One Specific Visit

Create a visit agenda with:

- Expected arrival date.
- Expected departure date.
- Where meetings will occur.
- Who will attend each meeting.
- What business topic each meeting covers.
- Confirmation that ordinary Dominican Republic operations continue from the Dominican Republic.
- Confirmation that Diana will depart after the visit.

Keep the visit short and tied to a real business purpose. Do not describe a broad open-ended need for her physical presence in the United States.

## Step 3 - Prepare The Support Packet

Use `travel-packet-checklist.md` for each visit. The packet should be organized around three issues:

1. Purpose of trip.
2. Intent to depart after the temporary visit.
3. Ability to pay trip costs.

The invitation letter can support the purpose of the visit, but official guidance says visitor-visa applicants qualify based on their own eligibility and ties abroad, not simply because a United States person invited them.

## Step 4 - Apply Or Renew Through Official Channels

Use official visa channels only:

- Complete the DS-160 through the official Consular Electronic Application Center.
- Follow the United States Embassy or Consulate instructions for the country where Diana applies.
- Bring required documents and any optional support packet to the interview if requested or useful.

Do not use third-party sites that impersonate official visa services.

## Step 5 - Travel Discipline

If Diana receives a visa and is admitted:

- Check the I-94/admission record after arrival.
- Calendar the admitted-until date immediately.
- Keep the visit inside the planned business purpose.
- Do not perform activities requiring a different legal status or authorization.
- Keep proof of departure and business agenda records in the private data room.

## Step 6 - After Each Visit

Save a short visit closeout memo outside sensitive public surfaces:

- Actual arrival/departure dates.
- Meetings held.
- Business topics reviewed.
- Decisions made.
- Confirmation that she returned to the Dominican Republic.
- Any follow-up tasks that will be performed from the Dominican Republic.
