## Working Notes

- A visitor visa allows a traveler to request admission; it does not guarantee entry.
- CBP determines admission and authorized stay at the port of entry.
- The I-94/admission record controls the authorized stay, not simply the visa expiration date.
- Official visitor-visa materials distinguish business consultation and contract negotiation from employment or productive labor physically performed in the United States.
