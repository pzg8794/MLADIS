- MLADIS LLC filing receipt and Articles packet.
- Operating Agreement.
- Initial Written Consent.
- Diana Founding Operations Pillar Acknowledgment and Services Understanding.
- MLADIS Bookings summary or business plan excerpt.

## Visit-Specific Records

- Business Visitor Invitation Letter with specific dates.
- Visit agenda with meeting topics.

- Proof of who will pay travel costs.
- Piter Zacari Garcia Bautista contact information.
- Any advisor/vendor meeting confirmations if applicable.

## Diana's Personal/Ties-Abroad Records

Store these outside Git or in private encrypted storage:

- Passport and visa documents.
- Proof of Dominican Republic residence.
- Proof of Dominican Republic family, property, business, employment, or other ties.
- Proof of ordinary operations and responsibilities in the Dominican Republic.
- Prior I-94/admission history if relevant.

## After Arrival

- Calendar departure before the admitted-until date.
- Keep activities inside the visit agenda and temporary business purpose.

## After Departure

- Save proof of departure.
- Save a short visit closeout memo.
- Record follow-up tasks to be performed from the Dominican Republic.
