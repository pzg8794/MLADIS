# MLADIS Business Visitor Records

Private planning records for temporary business-visitor support connected to MLADIS LLC and MLADIS Bookings.

These files are process guides and templates. They are not legal advice, immigration advice, tax advice, or a promise that a visa will be issued or that entry will be granted.

## Files

- `b1-business-visitor-process.md` - step-by-step process for Diana's possible temporary business visits.
- `business-visitor-invitation-letter-template.md` - reusable invitation letter template for a specific visit.
- `business-visit-policy.md` - internal MLADIS policy for non-U.S. business visitors.
- `travel-packet-checklist.md` - document checklist for each planned visit.
- `visa-history-review-private-template.md` - private review template; completed answers should stay outside Git.
- `safe-language-guide.md` - phrases and framing rules for immigration-safe business documentation.
- `source-links.md` - official source links used to build this package.

## Core Rule

Diana Sori Garcia Bautista may be documented as a Dominican Republic-based operational leader for MLADIS Bookings who may visit the United States temporarily for consultation, strategy, planning, and coordination. Do not describe a visit as authorization for employment or productive labor physically performed in the United States.
