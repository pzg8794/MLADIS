## Information needed before completing the response

- Current business mailing address.
- Current business physical address.
- Business telephone number.
- Entity type confirmation.
- Classification confirmation for federal and New York purposes.
- Owner legal name and owner type.
- Federal business identifier from the private record vault.
- Six-digit business activity code.
- Date the LLC began business in New York State.
- Total number of members or owners.
- Ownership percentage for each member or owner.
- Officer or authorized-signatory information, if applicable.
- Whether MLADIS LLC is a successor to any prior business entity.
- Whether any prior registrations need to transfer to the LLC.

## Working assumption to review

MLADIS LLC appears to be a New York domestic single-member LLC with one owner. Do not rely on this assumption when completing the response until Piter verifies it against the operating agreement, formation records, private identifier letter, and professional guidance.

## Recommended process

1. Complete the response using private records, not repo text.
2. Print or export the completed response.
3. Sign and date it by the authorized owner, member, or officer.
4. Fax it immediately using the number shown on the notice.
5. Save the confirmation in the private vault.
6. Optionally mail the same signed response as a backup.
7. Confirm receipt with the agency.
8. Update this tracker with the date sent, confirmation status, and any follow-up required.

## Important note

This tracker is an operating checklist, not professional advice. Confirm classification, business activity code, and registration implications with a qualified professional before relying on them for official filings.
