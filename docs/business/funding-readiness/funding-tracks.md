# Funding Tracks

Private planning note. Re-check official program rules before applying.

## Track 1 - Bank / CDFI / SBA-Backed Loan

Best for:

- Working capital.
- Software/platform buildout.
- Marketing.
- Property improvements.
- Booking operations expansion.

Needs before applying:

- EIN.
- Business bank account.
- Signed operating agreement.
- Revenue history and expense records.
- Business plan.
- Use-of-funds plan.
- Ability-to-repay story.
- Personal/business credit review.

Notes:

- SBA generally does not directly lend except disaster loans; SBA-backed loans are made through participating lenders.
- Lenders will care about repayment, cash flow, collateral/guarantees, owner credit, and clean records.

## Track 2 - NYC / NYS Financing And CDFI Support

Best for:

- Early small-business capital search.
- Help preparing loan documents.
- Connecting to local lenders.
- Possible programs for minority-owned businesses.

Needs before applying:

- EIN and bank account.
- Clean business profile.
- Basic financial package.
- Clear use of funds.

Notes:

- NYC SBS financing assistance and CDFI marketplaces can help find options, but lenders still underwrite.

## Track 3 - Grants

Best for:

- Specific eligible programs, not general startup money.
- R&D, technology commercialization, exports, community programs, workforce development, or agency-specific goals.

Needs before applying:

- EIN.
- SAM.gov UEI for many federal grants.
- Grants.gov account if using federal grant submissions.
- Program-specific narrative.
- Compliance capacity and reporting ability.

Notes:

- Do not chase generic grants. Start with fit: AI/automation, tourism, small-business growth, MWBE, export, community mission, or workforce training.

## Track 4 - NYC / NYS M/WBE Certification

Best for:

- Visibility to government buyers.
- Contracting opportunities.
- Targeted financing/training/networking resources.

Needs before applying:

- Proof the business is legally allowed to operate in NY.
- Ownership, operation, and control documentation.
- Owner identity/citizenship or permanent-resident documentation as required.
- Operating agreement.
- Tax returns when available.
- Bank documents when available.
- Owner resume and business capability statement.

Notes:

- Certification is not a grant by itself; it improves access and credibility for specific opportunities.

## Track 5 - Private Investors

Best for:

- Scaling the AI booking/automation framework.
- Building a broader hospitality software product.
- Expanding beyond one rental operation.

Needs before pitching:

- Clear company story: hospitality operations, AI booking framework, or both.
- Product demo or working platform.
- Metrics: bookings, revenue, inquiries, conversion, reviews, retention, costs.
- Financial forecast.
- Use-of-funds plan.
- Legal review before offering equity, revenue share, SAFE, convertible note, or other investment instrument.

Notes:

- Do not take private money until the operating agreement, ownership, securities-law path, and accounting treatment are reviewed.
