# MLADIS Bank, Credit, Loan, And Funding Guide

Prepared 2026-06-03. This is a planning guide, not legal, tax, accounting, or financial advice. Confirm terms directly with each bank, lender, or government program before applying.

## EIN Handling

The ready-for-signature governance packet does not need the EIN value. Do not add the EIN to:

- Operating Agreement.
- Initial Member Consent.
- Diana Founding Operations Pillar Acknowledgment.
- Public website, customer emails, or public-facing documents.

Use the EIN only in official bank, tax, CPA, payroll, payment processor, vendor tax, or government portal workflows. The private EIN reference is stored outside Git in the Google Drive vault documented in [private-records-manifest.md](../ein-and-banking/private-records-manifest.md).

## Funding Strategy For MLADIS

MLADIS should build financing in layers, from lowest-risk operating infrastructure toward credit and grants:

1. Verify and fund the approved Mercury business account, use it consistently,
   and keep clean monthly statements.
2. Add one business credit card or charge card used only for business expenses.
3. Build bookkeeping, monthly P&L, cash-flow forecast, and proof of revenue.
4. Apply for smaller working-capital products or CDFI microloans before larger loans.
5. Use SBA or state-backed loans only when we have a documented use of funds and repayment plan.
6. Treat grants as project-specific reimbursements or competitive programs, not guaranteed operating cash.

## Recommended Opening Sequence

### Phase 1 - Mercury Verification And Use

The Mercury account was approved on 2026-06-19. Verify that it is open and in
good standing, fund it if the first deposit is still outstanding, and confirm
that it provides the needed combination of:

- Easy onboarding for a new NY single-member LLC.
- Low/no monthly fee path.
- Good online banking and statement exports.
- Zelle/ACH/wire support if needed.
- Branch access in Queens/NYC.
- Ability to grow into credit card, line of credit, and SBA conversations.

Keep the private bank packet available if Mercury requests reverification:

- Filing receipt and Articles.
- Signed Operating Agreement.
- Signed Initial Member Consent.
- IRS EIN confirmation letter.
- Owner ID and proof of address if requested.

### Phase 2 - Credit Building

After the account is verified and being used:

- Use a business credit card only for predictable expenses: hosting, software, advertising tests, supplies, minor repairs.
- Pay in full every month.
- Keep receipts and match them in bookkeeping.
- Do not use business credit for owner personal expenses.
- Do not open multiple cards quickly.

### Phase 3 - First Financing Targets

Best early targets:

- CDFI microloan or NYS microloan for small improvements, working capital, repairs, marketing, or website/booking-system buildout.
- SBA microloan if under $50,000 is enough and a local intermediary fits.
- NYC SBS financing assistance to prepare the package before applying.

Later targets:

- SBA 7(a) once there is repayment history, revenue, and clean books.
- SBA 7(a) Working Capital Pilot only after one year of operating history and financial reporting discipline.
- NYS SSBCI / New York Forward-style programs after the bookkeeping packet and use-of-funds plan are ready.

## Credit And Loan Readiness Checklist

Before applying for credit or loans, prepare:

- Mercury business checking account open, current, funded, and being used.
- EIN confirmation letter in private vault.
- Signed Operating Agreement and Initial Member Consent.
- Bookkeeping categories and receipt storage.
- 3-6 months of clean statements once available.
- Revenue summary from Airbnb/direct booking.
- Use-of-funds plan: advertising, repairs, booking platform, furniture, insurance, deposits reserve, or software.
- Monthly repayment estimate and fallback plan.
- Owner credit review and debt schedule.
- Customer/review/occupancy metrics.
- Insurance and compliance status.

## Use Of Funds Ideas

Good uses:

- Booking platform and payment/deposit system hardening.
- Professional photography, listing optimization, and multilingual website content.
- Controlled advertising tests with measurable booking conversion.
- Repairs, safety upgrades, and guest-experience improvements.
- Cleaning/vendor operating reserves.
- Insurance, accounting, and compliance costs.

Avoid funding with debt unless there is a clear payback path:

- Untracked advertising experiments.
- Personal expenses.
- Large remodels before occupancy/revenue data supports the risk.
- Multiple software subscriptions without usage metrics.
