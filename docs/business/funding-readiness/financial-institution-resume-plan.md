## Phase 1 - Verify, Fund, And Operate Mercury

Goal: make the existing business account a verified, consistently used money
lane before any serious funding conversation.

### Owner Actions

1. Log in directly to Mercury and verify that the MLADIS LLC account is open
   and in good standing.
2. Make the first deposit if the account remains unfunded.
3. Download a bank-generated account-verification letter and every available
   monthly statement to the owner-only record.
4. Confirm that Stripe and any intended Airbnb payout method point to the
   correct MLADIS LLC account.
5. Decline overdraft credit, merchant cash advance, loan, or line of credit
   unless the owner explicitly decides otherwise in writing.

### Agent Actions

1. Maintain the account verification/control packet in [bank-account-opening-packet.md](../ein-and-banking/bank-account-opening-packet.md).
2. Open or stage the verified owner-only Drive record, but do not copy sensitive numbers into Git.
3. After current status, funding, and payout links are verified, update:
   - [../platform-account-update-checklist.md](../platform-account-update-checklist.md).
   - [funding-readiness-todo.md](funding-readiness-todo.md).
   - [data-room-index.md](data-room-index.md).

## Phase 6 - Talk To Banks, CDFIs, And SBA/NYC/NYS Support

Goal: start with advice and relationship-building, not desperate applications.

### Recommended Conversation Order

1. Primary business bank or fintech for checking.
2. NYC SBS financing assistance or NYC Funds Finder.
3. CDFI/microloan conversations.
4. SBA Lender Match research after the financial packet is ready.
5. Traditional bank/SBA-backed loan conversations when repayment ability is documented.

### Questions To Ask Every Lender

- What minimum time-in-business or revenue history do you require?
- What owner credit score range do you normally expect?
- Do you require collateral or a personal guarantee?
- What documents should MLADIS prepare before applying?
- What loan size is realistic based on the current financials?
- What are the interest rate, fees, prepayment rules, and default terms?
- Is this product fixed-payment, revenue-based, line-of-credit, or term debt?
- What would make MLADIS more approvable in 30, 60, or 90 days?

### Conversation Log

For each conversation, record:

- Date.
- Institution/contact.
- Product discussed.
- Requested amount range.
- Required documents.
- Eligibility notes.
- Risks/fees.
- Follow-up task.

Store private correspondence outside Git. A sanitized status summary may be committed if useful.

## Official Source Checks

Re-check these before submitting applications:

- [SBA Lender Match](https://www.sba.gov/funding-programs/loans/lender-match) - SBA says lenders expect a business plan, amount/use of funds, credit history, financial projections, collateral information, and industry experience.
- [SBA business plan guide](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/plan-your-business/write-your-business-plan) - use for business plan and projections structure.
- [NYC SBS financing assistance](https://nyc-business.nyc.gov/nycbusiness/business-services/financial-assistance//financing-assistance) - free help reviewing documents, calculating repayment terms, and connecting to lenders.
- [NYC financial assistance](https://nyc-business.nyc.gov/nycbusiness/business-services/financial-assistance) - current NYC financing programs and support.
- [SBA loans](https://www.sba.gov/funding-programs/loans) - current SBA-backed loan categories.
- [FinCEN BOI quick reference](https://www.fincen.gov/boi/quick-reference) - re-check BOI rules before relying on any exemption.
