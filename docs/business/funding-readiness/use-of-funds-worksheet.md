## Underwriting Narrative

Use this structure for lenders:

1. MLADIS has operating rental traction through existing listings.
2. Funds will not be used vaguely; they are tied to compliance, operations, revenue growth, and system buildout.
3. The business will separate funds, maintain records, and produce monthly reporting.
4. Direct-booking growth and repeat-guest campaigns should improve margin and reduce platform dependency over time.
5. AI/automation reduces admin load and improves customer response time.

## Questions To Answer Before Applying

- How much money do we actually need?
- What must be funded first to create revenue or reduce risk?
- What is the monthly repayment capacity?
- Which assets or contracts prove traction?
- Which expenses are one-time setup versus recurring?
- What happens if occupancy drops?
- What happens if direct booking grows slower than expected?
- What reporting can we provide monthly to a lender/investor?
