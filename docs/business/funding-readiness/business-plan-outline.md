## 3. Problem

Vacation-rental operators often rely on third-party platforms that limit direct customer relationships, control over deposits, automated support, customer analytics, and future cross-property reuse.

MLADIS is trying to solve:

- Direct booking without losing customer trust.
- Safer security-deposit handling.
- Better guest communication before and during booking.
- Cleaner operations reporting for owner/admin decisions.
- A reusable AI-assisted booking framework that can expand beyond one rental operation.

## 4. Solution

- AI agent support for customer questions and booking assistance.

## 12. Risks And Mitigations

Known risks:

- Platform dependency on Airbnb until direct-booking traction grows.
- Short-term rental compliance and tax obligations.
- Payment disputes, chargebacks, refunds, and damage claims.
- Risk of overstating completed title, lodging, revenue, or contractor
  authority before the G-101/G-102 legal transition is executed.
- AI/customer-data privacy and accuracy.

Mitigations:

- Keep records clean.
- Separate business funds.
- Use admin-confirmed bookings until calendar/pricing confidence improves.
- Maintain transparent terms and cancellation policies.
- Track consent for direct marketing.
- Review insurance, tax, and compliance with professionals.
