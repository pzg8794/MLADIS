Private planning note. Not legal, tax, or immigration advice.

## Current Decision

Do not add a family member only to improve visa chances. Add a member only when there is a real business reason, a clear contribution, a clear ownership percentage, and a reviewed tax/immigration plan.

## Can A Non-U.S. Person Be Added?

Generally, a New York LLC may have a non-U.S. member. If MLADIS later admits a member who lives in the Dominican Republic, the operating agreement should be amended before the admission is effective.

Before adding a non-U.S. member, confirm:

- Ownership percentage.
- Capital contribution or services contribution.
- Voting and management rights.
- Whether the LLC becomes multi-member for tax purposes.
- Whether the company must file partnership tax returns.
- Whether withholding/reporting applies to a foreign member.
- Whether the member needs an ITIN or other tax documentation.
- Whether the bank and payment processors allow the ownership/control structure.

## Immigration Reality Check

Ownership in a U.S. LLC does not by itself grant a visa, work authorization, or permission to live in the United States.

Potential paths to discuss with an immigration attorney:

- B-1 business visitor: may cover meetings, negotiations, consultations, and similar business activities, but not employment or productive labor physically performed in the United States.
- E-2 treaty investor: requires nationality of a treaty country and a substantial active investment. The Dominican Republic does not appear as an E treaty country on the State Department treaty-country table, and the Dominican Republic reciprocity page lists E-1 as `No Treaty`; verify E-2 directly before relying on this path.
- L-1 intracompany transferee: requires a qualifying U.S. and foreign organization relationship and qualifying employment abroad, typically at least one continuous year in the prior three years for the foreign company/affiliate.

Adding someone as an owner without a real operating role or investment can hurt credibility. For visa planning, the business facts should be truthful, documented, and consistent with the visa category.

## Safer Future Structure If She Helps From The Dominican Republic

If the goal is for a sister in the Dominican Republic to help the business before any U.S. visa strategy:

1. Keep MLADIS LLC single-member for now.
2. Keep using the archived contractor agreement, or sign an updated foreign services agreement, for work performed from the Dominican Republic.
3. Track payments and tax forms properly.
4. Only consider ownership later if she contributes capital, assets, or major operational value.
5. Get tax and immigration advice before changing ownership.
