# Business Bank Account Checklist

Prepare these before opening a MLADIS LLC business bank account.

## Likely Documents

- [ ] Articles of Organization or filing acknowledgement/receipt.
- [ ] Signed operating agreement.
- [ ] Initial member consent or banking resolution.
- [ ] EIN confirmation letter from IRS.
- [ ] Government ID for Piter Zacari Garcia Bautista.
- [ ] Business address and mailing address.
- [ ] Contact phone and email.
- [ ] Website/domain information, if requested.

## Controls To Set Up

- [ ] Separate MLADIS LLC income from personal funds.
- [ ] Use the business account for direct-booking income, deposits, refunds, operating expenses, software, hosting, ads, repairs, and cleaning.
- [ ] Keep a minimum reserve for refunds, chargebacks, and damage-deposit release timing.
- [ ] Export monthly bank statements to private records.
- [ ] Reconcile bank, payment processor, and booking records monthly.

## Payment Processor Notes

- Stripe and PayPal should use the LLC legal name and EIN once the EIN exists.
- Security deposits should be tracked separately from earned revenue.
- Holds, captures, releases, refunds, and disputes should create audit records in the MLADIS admin system.
