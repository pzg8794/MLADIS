# Starting Bookkeeping Categories

Draft chart of accounts for MLADIS LLC. Review with a CPA before tax filing.

## Income

- Airbnb/platform rental income.
- Direct-booking lodging income.
- Cleaning fees collected.
- Extra guest fees.
- Damage/penalty income, only when actually retained.
- Other service or software income.

## Liabilities And Held Funds

- Security deposit holds.
- Customer deposits not yet earned.
- Refunds payable.
- Chargeback reserve.
- Sales/lodging/tourism taxes payable, if applicable.

## Cost Of Revenue

- Cleaning services.
- Laundry.
- Supplies and consumables.
- Repairs and maintenance.
- Replacement items and damage repairs.
- Utilities allocated to rental operations.
- Platform fees.
- Payment processor fees.

## Operating Expenses

- Website hosting.
- Cloudflare/domain/DNS.
- Software subscriptions.
- OpenAI/API/AI infrastructure.
- Email/Google Workspace.
- Accounting/bookkeeping tools.
- Legal and professional fees.
- Insurance.
- Advertising and promotion.
- Photography and media.
- Office/admin expenses.
- Publication/compliance costs.

## Owner Equity

- Owner contributions.
- Owner distributions.
- Owner reimbursements.

## Mission/Donation Tracking

- Charity donations paid by MLADIS LLC.
- Customer-directed donation pass-throughs, if ever collected.
- Promotional discounts tied to mission campaigns.

Keep customer-directed donation funds clearly separated from company charitable donations so reporting stays clean.
