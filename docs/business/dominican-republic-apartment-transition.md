## Required Legal Structure

Dominican counsel must document the lawful structure separately for each
apartment. The two principal routes to evaluate are:

1. A registered sale, contribution, or other title transfer to MLADIS LLC,
   after current ownership, liens, mortgage status, transfer tax, company
   qualification, and every required lender/Fiduciaria consent are resolved.
2. A signed lease, management, or operating agreement that grants MLADIS LLC
   enforceable control and revenue rights while individual title and financing
   remain in place, if counsel concludes that direct title transfer is not yet
   lawful, economical, or lender-approved.

Changing an Airbnb profile, W-9, payout method, website, or company email does
not replace either legal route.

## Required Evidence And Approvals

- current certified title and legal-status/encumbrance report for G-101 and
  G-102;
- current owner/borrower, payoff, mortgage, lien, and lender-consent status;
- Fiduciaria or seller obligations still affecting final title delivery;
- company/member resolutions and notarized transfer, lease, management, or
  operating instruments selected by counsel;
- apostilled New York formation and authority records, legalized Spanish
  translations, and Dominican representation documents where required;
- foreign-company/branch registration or another lawful local structure;
- RNC, Registro Mercantil, invoicing, withholding, ITBIS, income-tax, and
  information-return treatment;
- written condominium/HOA confirmation that the intended short-term-rental or
  lodging use is permitted;
- MITUR/RENATUR lodging registration, license, inspection, and local land-use
  approvals determined applicable by counsel;
- current property, contents, civil-liability, business-interruption, and
  short-term-rental coverage naming the correct owners/operators; and
- documented assignment or transition of Airbnb listings, bookings, payouts,
  contracts, deposits, expenses, and revenue to MLADIS LLC.

The condominium rules describe the apartments as residential, require leases
to bind tenants to the rules, and state that no exploitation other than family
dwelling use may be established. Because that language may conflict with
transient lodging, written HOA and Dominican legal review is required before
MLADIS represents the use as approved.

MITUR's published checklist applies to first and renewal operating licenses for
the activity `HOSPEDAJE`. It includes tax and social-security good-standing
certifications, current Registro Mercantil, authority and ONAPI records,
property-use evidence, bank references, civil-liability insurance, land-use
approval, inspection, and occupancy-platform compliance. Counsel must confirm
the correct applicant and exact license category for these two apartments.
