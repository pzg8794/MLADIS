# Publication Completion Checklist

Use this after the Queens County Clerk assigns the designated newspapers.

## Step 1 - Get Designations

- [x] Save NYBE filing acknowledgement or Department of State filing receipt.
- [x] Send publication designation request to Queens County Clerk at `QCC-LLC@nycourts.gov`.
  - Sent from `garcp37@mladis.com` on 2026-06-03 with `MLADIS_LLC-FilingReceipt-And-Articles.pdf` attached.
- [ ] Save the County Clerk designation response/letter.
- [ ] Record designated daily newspaper: ______________________________
- [ ] Record designated weekly newspaper: _____________________________

## Step 2 - Place Notices

- [ ] Send notice draft to the designated daily newspaper.
- [ ] Confirm publication cost and payment method.
- [ ] Confirm first publication date.
- [ ] Send notice draft to the designated weekly newspaper.
- [ ] Confirm publication cost and payment method.
- [ ] Confirm first publication date.

## Step 4 - Collect Affidavits

- [ ] Receive affidavit of publication from daily newspaper.
- [ ] Receive affidavit of publication from weekly newspaper.
- [ ] Check that both affidavits use the exact legal name and correct publication dates.
- [ ] Keep copies in private records.

## Step 5 - File Certificate Of Publication

- [ ] Complete Certificate of Publication for Domestic Limited Liability Company.
- [ ] Attach both affidavits.
- [ ] Include $50 filing fee payable to Department of State.
- [ ] Mail or otherwise submit to NY DOS Division of Corporations.
- [ ] Save filed Certificate of Publication confirmation.
