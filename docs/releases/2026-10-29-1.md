## Known limitations and release behavior

- Google Cloud account was reauthenticated through its existing identity; no new account or permission scope was introduced.
- The existing dedicated agent SMTP credential is rejected by Google (SMTP 535 and IMAP authentication failure). The runbooks and approved runtime sources were checked; no replacement was found. No credential was guessed, reset, generated, logged or committed.
- Signup is disabled by default. `/offers/` truthfully displays temporary unavailability and accepts no email submission until confirmation delivery is restored and `AudienceCampaignSettings.signup_enabled` is explicitly enabled.
- The audience backend, 10% coupon flow and unsubscribe are implemented/tested, but no production subscriber, coupon or campaign is created by deployment.
- Future promotional sends also require the operator to set a verified public business postal address. No private legal address is published by this release.
- The new guide has no marketing/signup/payment links, no fixed checkout time and no deposit/contract acceptance. Legacy direct-booking rules and reservation terms are unchanged.
- No Airbnb guest messages or campaigns are sent; no existing Airbnb contacts are imported into marketing.
