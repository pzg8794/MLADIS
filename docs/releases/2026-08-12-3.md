## Scope

This release completes the receipt/document trail for both sides of a guest
transaction:

- deliver the guest-facing receipt to the transaction customer;
- deliver the administrative copy to the configured MLADIS communication
  recipients;
- retain the customer document in the guest account;
- retain the administrative document in the MLADIS admin workspace;
- keep both copies linked to the same invoice and reservation evidence;
- preserve the no-real-charge verification boundary used by the release canary.

No database model, migration, OAuth setting, middleware, payment capture,
refund, or deposit movement changed in this release.

## Functional Claim Boundary

This release may be described as operational only for the checks and workflows
whose evidence is recorded here and in the post-deployment verification report.
It must not be described as proving every possible platform function unless the
full inventory and evidence standard in
`docs/qa/full-site-functional-verification-plan.md` is complete.

The following provider/destructive operations are intentionally excluded from
the release canary:

- real Stripe charge, capture, or refund;
- PayPal checkout while approved sandbox credentials are unavailable;
- irreversible production guest communications other than the controlled
  receipt evidence above;
- destructive production state transitions without a reversible canary.
