Your email, your choice

The offers form is optional. You can request a 10% welcome code, occasional seasonal offers, or both. It is separate from reservations and never a condition of a stay.

What we keep

We store your email address, selected purposes, the consent wording and version, request and confirmation dates, subscription status, and any issued code. We also keep short-lived keyed hashes and counters to limit abuse; we do not put your email address in public URLs.

How we use it

We send an email verification link. Nothing activates until you confirm on that page. If you select seasonal offers, we use your confirmed address for those messages. Requesting a discount alone does not subscribe you to recurring marketing.

Access and retention

MLADIS staff with authorized access can manage the audience. Records are stored in our protected booking database and operational backups/object store. Our existing email provider processes messages for delivery. We do not publish or sell the audience.

Stop or change your preferences

Use the unsubscribe link in your confirmation email, confirmation page or subsequent offers. Unsubscribing stops seasonal offers and cancels pending signup requests; it does not change reservations. We retain the minimum suppression and consent history needed to respect your choice and prevent duplicate welcome codes.
