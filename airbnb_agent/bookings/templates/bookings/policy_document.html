1. What this hold is

The refundable damage deposit is an authorization hold used to protect MLADIS, the property owner, and future guests from property damage, missing items, excessive cleaning, rule violations, residential fines, access-device replacement, and other costs caused during a reservation.

2. When it may be captured

The hold is not automatically captured. MLADIS may capture all or part of the hold only when there is a documented reason connected to the stay, including property damage, missing items, broken appliances or furniture, smoke or odor remediation, excessive cleaning, lost keys or access devices, unauthorized guests, unauthorized parties, amenity misuse, parking or building fines, or other violations of the property rules.

3. How MLADIS documents issues

When possible, MLADIS will use photos, messages, staff notes, invoices, vendor quotes, receipts, building notices, cleaning reports, or maintenance records to document the issue. The amount captured should be reasonably connected to the cost of remediation, replacement, fines, vendor work, or administrative recovery required because of the incident.

4. If costs exceed the hold

If damage or a rule violation exceeds the deposit amount, MLADIS may seek the remaining balance through invoice, payment request, platform claim, legal claim, or another lawful recovery method.

5. Release of the hold

If no issue is documented, the hold should be released according to the payment processor and card issuer timeline.

By accepting these terms, the guest confirms that they reviewed the property rules, understands that violations may result in charges, and agrees to this Damage Deposit Hold policy.
